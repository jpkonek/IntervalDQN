## Mechanism verdicts

### Path-dependent wins: pre-existence test via issuance-time CBP conflict-payment sign + decaying per-pair flag: AMEND

The RULING's concept (W_rec gated on pre-existing danger; self-made danger pays nothing and already costs) survives and is buildable. The drafted INSTRUMENT does not: verified in code, paid_conf is a single sector-aggregate scalar (shaping_terms sums all matched pairs, bluebird_controller_dqn.py:485-491) so 'per-pair flag' has no defined source; pair_conflict_f has compact support (f=0 for d>=15 nm, :439-443, and gate mode — the default, :219 — is blind to FL clearances at any lateral range), so the sign is identically zero for exactly the endangerment that matters (outside-ramp issuance, persistent-clearance closure); after issuance the CBP counterfactual inherits the clearance, so every no-command drift step pays 0 and resolving commands pay POSITIVE — the causation channel is invisible to the payment channel by construction; and no decay length L is consistent (steep in-ramp needs L>=8, shallow 10-degree burn needs L>=23, any L>=23 disarms nearly every pair at the measured 0.5-0.9 cmds/step, silently collapsing W_rec>0 to de facto 0). Amendment: primary test becomes the per-pair cbp_noop_snapshot causal test at band onset ('would this pair have entered the band under NOOP within H steps?' — existing :620 machinery, side-effect-free), which is matched to the persistent-clearance causation channel and needs no L. The sign channel is retained only as supplementary telemetry with per-pair decomposition and a pre-registered flag-coverage measurement. Not REJECT because the ruling is satisfied by the replacement operationalization; not PASS because nothing of the drafted mechanism ships as primary.

### Sub-episode reward accounting: windows collapse at recovery boundaries, W_rec as arc ending, floored cross-boundary bootstrap realized + gamma^m * max(Q_target, 0): AMEND

Segmentation per ruling 2 is buildable only under a fully pinned contract the ruling leaves open, and one element of the ruling as written is unsound. (a) The floor is never a 'known anchor': in the doom regime it transiently reproduces anchor-0 arithmetic (but strictly dominates the unfloored bootstrap there — one-sided pessimistic error 0..21 vs 5..60 — and self-extinguishes); in the repaired regime it is a state-selective optimism injector (E[max(Q,0)] >= max(E[Q],0)), largest at the true-V~0 restraint states this round targets, ~27 gamma^m-discounted at boundary-adjacent windows decaying to ~9 at m=36, invisible to a paid-reward-only pump audit, and — composed with the named-pair resolution predicate and the pre-existence test's shared sub-15 nm blind spot — able to launder an imminent -50 out of up to 36 window targets for a seeding action that simultaneously earns the win and passes the sign test. (b) The machinery has no home for the third collapse mode: the 8-field row carries no kind tag, terminal collapse pushes disc=0 empty-state rows, flush-reuse stamps next_a_idx=0 and would corrupt B3's censored identifier; W_rec has no legal payment site (raw addition crashes the reconciliation assert :2041-2045; the delivery-template route passes it but leaks W_rec into CF branch pricing and destroys the ruling-3 belief-vs-payment decomposition). (c) The boundary is unpinned between per-pair (bootstraps on states with a live second conflict — wrong in SIGN) and global (may never fire under measured policies — silently inert). Amended design: terminal_kind thread with armed-recovery/unarmed-recovery, armed-only collapse (unarmed arcs run through, preserving the realized tail of self-made danger), global boundary with a fire-rate counter and a revert-biased trigger (A4 relief is partially debunked — flush_censored fires per time-limit ending regardless), no-bootstrap deferred collapse as default, floor (if kept) per-row/both-bounds/real-action-stamped with a target-side audit arm, W_rec paid in run_episode under its own budget column. Buildable, but two scope changes need JK sign-off (see jk_items).

### NOOP tie-break tolerance in action selection (threshold ~0.1 at fee scale): AMEND

Ruling 4's mechanism ships, but the drafted 0.1 constant cannot: the measured best-non-NOOP-over-NOOP margins (+0.7..+2.9 in M2-XL, with a c*(width difference) term at widths 5-15 that alone exceeds 0.1) sit far above it, while any threshold large enough to bind (>=~0.7) sits above the entire genuine decision-signal band (CMD_COST 0.1, paid_conflict 0.01-0.14/step, deferred-fee margin ~0.003) and would erase the within-window signal for WHEN to act. Critically, the refutation of the strongest never-fires claim stands FOR the right reason: the M2-XL margins are doom-biased (Q(NOOP) reads -5..-38 vs truth ~0 on 12d-final) and measure the pathology being repaired, not the residual noise the tolerance targets — so 'unbuildable' cannot be pre-judged from them either. The amendment converts the threshold into a two-sided pre-registered measurement (post-repair noise band as lower bound at the A1+B-only checkpoint; minimum GT resolving-command margin as upper bound), with BOTH outcomes pre-registered symmetrically, including a JK-signed 'unbuildable as raw-score threshold — reduce selection noise instead' branch that cannot become a round-4 reward lever by default. Placement is pinned (raw pre-bonus scores inside select_candidate only — the count bonus, scale 0.5 vs threshold 0.1, is added before the argmax at :1441 and would otherwise override the tolerance 5x during training then hand back control on bonus decay, mechanically forging C's belief-repair prediction), a stratum guard keeps it off in-band states, and the C instruments are locked against it by regression test plus dual-mode (tolerance-off) reporting so the command-rate bars stay readable.
