# ROUND-3 DESIGN REVIEW SYNTHESIS (20 July 2026)

25-agent adversarial review of ROUND3_DESIGN.md: 18 findings, 15 survived refutation. Every section AMEND.

## Section verdicts

- **A1: AMEND** — Value semantics are genuinely exact in spawn-0 micros, but the completion check is unspecified: it must be delivered_count == scenario roster, evaluated strictly AFTER the violation branch (the real corner is the OUT_SECTOR step where an undelivered aircraft leaves obs on the same step sector_excursion fires), with a terminal assert.
- **A2/A3: AMEND** — The concept stands per JK's ruling but every drafted mechanism is replaced: the anchor-0 segment terminal states a false continuation value (measured V(s_rec) ~ 0..+22, not 0) and orphans in-flight delivery credit; the sector-wide <2nm predicate is unsatisfiable or purchasable under persistent clearances; guard (ii) is vacuous (a win ends the very episode the counter is scoped to) and guard (i) is bypassed by command persistence while disarming exactly the endogenous-onset risk episodes T2/T6 showed dominate; and the pump audit's -50 cost floor is wrong (true floor ~0.3-1.0/cycle, so W_rec=5 nets +4/cycle).
- **A4: AMEND** — Its premise is factually wrong — flush_censored fires once per time-limit ending regardless of A2 segmentation (only A1 completions reduce the count, and the tail flush is up to 36 rows under production nstep=36) — and the review picks the bootstrap-magnitude cap, which is also required as a companion if A2 ever uses a target-net boundary bootstrap.
- **B1: AMEND** — Both cap semantics are biased for a time-blind net (terminal-cap teaches near-limit geometry is free; censored-cap re-imports the doom tail T2 measured), so branches crossing the ENV time limit are EXCLUDED, with the ~36% coverage cost at nstep=36 pre-registered and branch-side A2 semantics deferred as designed future work.
- **B2: AMEND** — B2 is implementable as drafted but class-blindly: push routes on disc==0.0 alone and the 8-tuple carries no win/violation tag or age stamp, so A2/A1 win terminals would capture the 3x boost built for sparse -50 anchors and dilute the violation signal ~16x — the schema extension the age cap already forces must carry a terminal_kind tag threaded from windower-collapse, with per-class boost/eviction policy and a violation-row batch floor.
- **B3: AMEND** — 'Re-measure, only fix if material' has no metric, null, threshold, or scheduled slot, and the quantity is a self-feeding loop, so a post-repair snapshot reads immaterial by construction — the decision rule (censored-vs-realized target gap, 30-init null, trend criterion with pre-fixed k and N) must be pre-registered in the doc now with an explicit build-order slot.
- **C: AMEND** — As drafted the prediction confirms in every world: A2's resolution machinery is itself a command-price channel (commanding delays or forfeits the win, up to 50x CMD_COST), the Q(NOOP) rise is partly guaranteed by B2/A4 arithmetic, the failure branch omits the selection-noise regime (NOOP must strictly beat all 5N heads to win argmax, real margin only ~0.1), and neither half has a threshold, instrument, or noise band.
- **D: AMEND** — D3/D4 stand, but the battery contains no instrument for the round's biggest new surface (A2 recovery behavior — M6 plus armed-win telemetry is mandatory), and D1's dual-basis GT is silently net-dependent on the frozen side, making per-checkpoint recompute cost and null-band validity unspecified, with no staleness governance on bands or GT tables.
- **E: AMEND** — The D -> B1 -> A1 -> B2 -> A2/A3 backbone survives (the direct ordering attack was refuted), but surviving amendments force insertions: the C arm-1 read at the A1+B-only checkpoint before A2/A3, the B2 schema/tagging change landing before any win rows exist, the two-direction pump audit as a JK-read gate, and an explicit B3 measurement step before the battery rerun.

## JK decision items

- Does W_rec = 0 (or <= 0.1) still honor your 'recovery is a win' ruling? The review's strongest result is that the win must exist as a LABEL with a correctly-priced anchor, not as a bonus: any bonus large enough to notice is farmable (worst-case pump cost is ~0.3-1.0 per cycle, not 50, so +5 nets ~+4/cycle), and every anti-farming guard drafted around it is either vacuous or disarms the win exactly when it's needed. Recommended default: W_rec = 0, win logged and instrumented (M6). If you want a positive bonus, it is capped by the measured pump audit, and you read that audit before A2 ships.

- Segmentation default: the draft ends the training episode at each recovery with anchor 0. That anchor is provably false (a recovered sector still holds 0..+22 of future value, state-dependent) and it orphans delivery credit in flight — the exact credit NSTEP was raised to reach. Recommended default: NO segmentation — recovery is a logged win event, credit flows through. Segmentation survives only as an option (with a floored bootstrap boundary target, never 0) if you decide the A4-reduction is worth the measured orphaned-delivery cost. Your call because it changes what 'episode' means for every bar you pre-registered.

- No-new-lever ruling boundary: A2's resolution machinery inherently taxes commanding (a lateral command delays or forfeits the win — up to 50x the command fee). The pre-registered W_rec=0 arm separates belief repair from payment-for-restraint. You must rule in advance: if command rate falls ONLY when W_rec > 0, is that an acceptable outcome or a violation of your no-command-penalty ruling by the back door? The review recommends treating it as a violation and reporting it as such.

- Pre-commit to the third C outcome: even with values fully repaired, NOOP can lose the action lottery on pure prediction noise (its true margin is only the 0.1 fee, and it must strictly beat every candidate head). If measurements route there, the fix is a tie-break tolerance in action selection, NOT a new reward lever — meaning a failed 'command rate falls' prediction does NOT automatically green-light lever design in round 4. Sign off on that fork now so the round-3 verdict can't be argued after the fact.

- Ground-truth budget: the training-basis GT is net-dependent and costs ~4 minutes of wall clock per 20-state table per checkpoint. Recommended: full-cadence tracking on the NOOP basis, training-basis tables at only ~4 checkpoints (init/quarter/half/final). Accept the coarser training-basis trajectory, or fund the full rebuild (hours per arm).

- Pre-registration constants to fix before any run: B3's trend window k and start episode N; the C noise floor (falls <= 0.05 in occupied-step command rate are noise, calibrated from the no-repair drift you already saw between rounds 1 and 2); and the M6 fallback if no geometry shows a low-enough natural violation rate.
